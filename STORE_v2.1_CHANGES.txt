OneTime Labs Store v2.1.0 — Storefront Redesign

- Removed the oversized marketing hero from the store home page.
- Rebuilt the header around commerce: brand, search, account, seller link, cart placeholder, and department navigation.
- Added shop-by-department cards for RAM, hardware, storage, software, and selling.
- Hardware inventory now appears as a normal storefront shelf.
- Empty hardware inventory is shown as a compact empty shelf instead of a marketing landing page.
- Software remains a dedicated storefront shelf.
- Added a compact seller callout below product shelves.
- Store search routes customers directly into the Hardware catalog.
- Marketplace/seller/admin functionality from v2.0.1 is retained.
